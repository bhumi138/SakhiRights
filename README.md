SakhiRights is a deterministic, local-language legal navigator designed to help women understand their workplace rights and take actionable steps against harassment.  
The Problem: Currently, only 1 in 10 women in India report workplace harassment. The primary barriers are dense legal jargon, language exclusivity, and a lack of accessible, neutral guidance. Existing solutions—such as costly lawyers, generic English-only chatbots, or employer-controlled HR portals—often leave women without actionable support.  
Our Solution: SakhiRights bridges this gap by providing trusted, explainable legal guidance in the user's native language. Rather than relying on a generic, probability-based AI chatbot that might hallucinate or guess answers, this platform uses a structured, rule-based decision tree rooted entirely in the POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Act. This ensures complete traceability to specific legal clauses and provides users with transparent, explainable recommendations.  

Key Features: 
1. Multi-Language Interface: Seamless support for 10+ regional languages, including Hindi, Tamil, and Bengali.  Structured
2. Decision Tree: Guides users step-by-step from incident description to actionable reporting options without using complex legal jargon.  3. Automated Complaint Generation: Auto-generates a formal, ready-to-submit POSH complaint draft (in PDF or DOCX formats) based on the specific facts of the incident.
4. Privacy & Anonymity: Offers an anonymous reporting mode to protect user identity throughout the legal process.
5. Resource Directory: Centralized access to HR contacts, escalation paths, and NGO support networks.
How It Works: 
1. Recognize: The user opens the platform and describes the incident in simple, everyday language.
2. Navigate: The system identifies if the incident qualifies under the POSH Act and explains the user's exact rights in plain language.
3. Report: The user selects their preferred reporting path (Internal Complaints Committee, external authority, or anonymous reporting).
4. Submit: SakhiRights generates a pre-filled complaint draft, which the user can review, edit, and submit with confidence.


Impact: SakhiRights directly aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goal 5 (Gender Equality). Designed for women employees across SMEs, colleges, NGOs, and corporate sectors, the platform aims to measurably increase reporting rates, accelerate internal resolution processes, and expand legal awareness across underserved communities.
